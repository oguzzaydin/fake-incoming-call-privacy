Data stored on your device

Your settings, saved callers (call presets), scheduled calls and call history are stored locally on your device. We do not upload them to our own servers. They stay until you delete them in the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.

Third-party services

The app uses these Google services, which may collect and process data such as device and advertising identifiers, diagnostics and crash logs, app interactions, and ad data:

  • Firebase Analytics (usage statistics)
  • Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports)
  • Google AdMob (ads)

Data held by these services is kept according to their own retention policies. See the Google Privacy Policy, how Google uses data from apps that use its services, and Firebase privacy information.

We do not sell your personal data.

Permissions

  • Notifications, exact alarms and full-screen intent: used only to show the simulated incoming call at the time you choose, including on the lock screen.
  • Text-to-speech: the optional voice after you answer uses your device's speech engine.

Ads and consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

Where required by law, you are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalized ads.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under GDPR, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to processing of your data. Contact us at boaapps.dev@gmail.com.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.
